Purpose of Church Membership
  • It gives you ownership and a greater commitment to the work of the local church.
  • It gives you the opportunity to be part of the decision-making process as we work together for a positive ministry emphasis.
  • It gives you the opportunity to serve in leadership that benefits both yourself and the local body of believers.
As we experience God's grace to us in Christ and better understand His glory, we become more like Him and are motivated to keep growing throughout our lives. The following spiritual practices are commitments we make as members that reflect our desire to be transformed by God's grace into fully devoted followers of Jesus Christ.
Membership Commitments
❤️ Love God
a)WORSHIP: I will give priority to corporate worship, whether in-person or online, engaging with my mind and heart.
b)WORD & PRAYER: I will spend time regularly reading the Bible and in personal prayer, including digital devotionals.
🌱 Grow in Christ
a)FELLOWSHIP: I will connect into a Home Fellowship Group, Youth Group, or Discipleship Circle.
b)PURSUE HOLINESS: Relying on the Holy Spirit's power, especially in relationships, work, and digital life.
c)DISCOVER MY CALLING Youth I am open to exploring my gifts through serving and mentoring.
🤝 Serve the Church
a)INVEST IN KCMC: Use time, talents, and skills to serve; give generously through traditional or digital means.
b)PROTECT KCMC: Live in harmony, being positive in speech and action offline/online, seeking biblical reconciliation.
🌍 Reach the World
a)WITNESS IN WORD: Share the Gospel with my home, school, workplace, community, and digital networks.
b)WITNESS INDEED: Adorn the Gospel with loving acts of service, youth-led outreach, and social justice.
c)DIGITAL DISCIPLESHIP Youth Be a light on social media, representing Christ with integrity.
