Purpose of Church Membership
  • It gives you ownership and a greater commitment to the work of the local church.
  • It gives you the opportunity to be part of the decision-making process as we work together for a positive ministry emphasis.
  • It gives you the opportunity to serve in leadership that benefits both yourself and the local body of believers.
As we experience God's grace to us in Christ and better understand His glory, we become more like Him and are motivated to keep growing throughout our lives. The following spiritual practices are commitments we make as members that reflect our desire to be transformed by God's grace into fully devoted followers of Jesus Christ.
Membership Commitments
❤️ Love God
a) WORSHIP: I will strive to increasingly love God with all that I am. As one important means of loving God, I will give priority to corporate worship, whether in-person or online, engaging with my mind and heart.
b) WORD & PRAYER: I will strive to spend time regularly reading the Bible and in personal prayer, including through digital devotionals or prayer apps if helpful.
🌱 Grow in Christ
a) PARTICIPATE IN FELLOWSHIP: I will connect into a Home Fellowship Group (HF), Youth Group, or Discipleship Circle to help build others up and grow toward maturity in Christ.
b) PURSUE HOLINESS: I will strive to live daily in a way that pleases God, relying on the Holy Spirit's power, especially in my relationships, studies/work, and digital life.
c) DISCOVER MY CALLING Youth I am open to exploring my gifts and passions through serving, mentoring, and training opportunities in the church.
🤝 Serve the Church
a) INVEST IN KCMC: I will use the time, talents, and skills God has given me to serve others in our church family and build up the body. I will give generously, cheerfully, and consistently to God through our church, whether through traditional or digital means.
b) PROTECT KCMC: I will strive to live in harmony and unity with others, being positive in speech and action, both offline and online, and seeking biblical reconciliation when conflicts arise.
🌍 Reach the World
a) WITNESS IN WORD: I will share the life-changing message of the Gospel with those in my home, school, workplace, community, and digital networks.
b) WITNESS INDEED: I will adorn the Gospel with loving acts of service and sacrifice, including through youth-led outreach and social justice initiatives.
c) DIGITAL DISCIPLESHIP Youth I will seek to be a light on social media and online platforms, representing Christ with integrity, kindness, and truth.
